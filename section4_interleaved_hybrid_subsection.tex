The new measurements use the single-processor interleaving
of Algorithm~\ref{alg:hybrid}, not the previously benchmarked
two-thread race. The deterministic routine is resumable through
an explicit BFS/DFS state machine; exact and randomized routines
alternate equal fixed budgets of elementary steps.
Randomized sampling wins on the cycle-rich graphs, giving
$2.79\times$, $4.06\times$, $10.80\times$, and $2.15\times$
speedups on A128c, A256c, C128c, and D16c, respectively.
Sparse A128r, A256r, and B128r return exact counts but suffer
significant state-machine and scheduling overhead. The
empirical ten-run MARE may exceed the expected $8\%$ bound.
These preliminary results establish no uniform wall-clock
speedup and should be expanded before final publication.
